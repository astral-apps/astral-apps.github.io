Privacy Policy – Wapsio

Effective date: 30 September 2026. Publisher: Astral Apps, Pune, Maharashtra, India. Contact: quizzillagame@gmail.com.

1. Summary

Wapsio ("the app") helps you organise information about a consumer problem on your own phone.

  • Your cases stay on your phone, encrypted. We (the publisher) never receive them. There is no account system.
  • The free version shows ads on the case list and in optional unlock videos, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads never appear inside your case, message or next-steps screens, and no case content is ever given to advertisers.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Pro backups go only to your own Google Drive, encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

2. What you enter and where it is stored

You may enter: seller or platform name, order or booking reference, amount paid, dates, a description, the outcome you want, timeline notes, reminder labels, family profile names and, optionally, your name for letters. You may attach references to photos, PDFs, screenshots, emails, chats and recordings.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Text fields are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held by Android Keystore that cannot be exported.

Attachments: the app keeps a reference to each file you pick, plus its name, type, size, date added and a SHA-256 fingerprint. Photos taken with the in-app "Take photo" option are saved in the app's private storage.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show:

  • a banner on the case list, and
  • optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to unlock a case's Complaint Pack.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect: your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), device and app information, and ad interactions. This is governed by Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The app never sends case text, order IDs, amounts, merchant names, attachments or messages to Google or anyone else for advertising.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings and privacy → Ad privacy choices when it is offered. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Buying Pro removes all ads.

4. Purchases

Purchases ("Unlock this case" and "Pro") are processed by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google shares with us only what is needed to deliver the purchase (an order ID, the product and purchase status). The purchase token and the case it unlocks are stored on your phone. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

5. Google Drive backup (Pro, optional)

If you turn on backup, the app asks for Google's permission to use a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). The app cannot see any of your other Drive files. Backups are encrypted on your phone with a passphrase you choose (PBKDF2 plus AES-256-GCM) before upload. We do not receive the backup or the passphrase and cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

6. What leaves your device

  • Ad requests, as described in section 3 (free version only).
  • Purchase requests to Google Play.
  • Encrypted backups to your own Google Drive, only if you use backup.
  • Anything you choose to share yourself: messages, PDFs, ZIP evidence packs, JSON exports. Exported files are not encrypted.

Android system backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app's data.

7. Analytics

Off by default. This version contains no analytics service. If one is added in future, it will receive only fixed event names and never case content. This policy will be updated first.

8. Permissions

Notifications (Android 13+): to show reminders you set. Internet: for ads, purchases and Drive backup. The app does not request SMS, call log, contacts, location, accessibility, notification access, broad storage or camera permissions.

9. Your controls

Export: Settings and privacy → Export my data. Delete: Settings and privacy → Delete all data (removes cases, photos, generated files, reminders, profiles and settings, and destroys the encryption key; purchases stay linked to your Google account). Delete a Drive backup: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → this app → Delete hidden app data. Uninstalling removes all local data.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 18, and ads are not configured for child audiences.

11. Official services

Links to the National Consumer Helpline, e-Jagriti and the cyber-crime portal open in your browser or dialer. The app is not affiliated with them.

12. Your rights and grievances (India)

For questions or grievances under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact our Grievance Officer: Anand N, quizzillagame@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.

13. Changes

Changes will be published in the app and on the Play Store listing, with a new effective date.
